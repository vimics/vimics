Workflow

How a case runs

  1. 01{{I:radiology}}

    CT data

    You send the patient's CT as a DICOM series.

  2. 02{{I:view_in_ar}}

    3D planning

    We segment the anatomy and plan trajectories, cuts or implant fit on the 3D model.

  3. 03{{I:precision_manufacturing}}

    Design and production

    The guide or implant is designed on the plan and produced by Additive Manufacturing.

  4. 04{{I:surgical}}

    Surgery

    You receive the guide or implant for the planned procedure.

In focus

Patient-specific pedicle screw guides

Planned on the patient's CT

Screw entry points, angles and lengths are set on the 3D model of the patient's own vertebrae.

One sleeve per trajectory

Each drill sleeve carries one planned trajectory. The guide body is shaped to the patient's vertebral surface.

Cervical to lumbosacral

Guides are designed for cervical, thoracolumbar and lumbosacral segments.

Materials

What the parts are made of

Implants

  • Produced from Ti6Al4V titanium alloy by Additive Manufacturing.
  • Porous lattice regions can be built into the implant design.
  • Designs can accept locking screws.

Surgical guides

  • Printed in a biocompatible guide resin.
  • Autoclavable.
